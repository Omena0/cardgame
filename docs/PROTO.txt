
*on game join*
c2s: JOIN_GAME <username>
s2c: PLAYERS <p1>,<p2>,...

*on player pressed ready button*
c2s: READY

*after all clients ready*
s2c: START_GAME

*on visible cards update*
s2c: VISIBLE <player name> <visible1> <visible2> <visible3>

*on hand update*
s2c: HAND <hand1> <hand2> <hand3> <hand4> <hand5>

*on client card reorder*
c2s: REORDER <hand1> <hand2> <hand3> <hand4> <hand5>

*on player done with swapping cards*
c2s: REORDER_DONE

*on all clients ready*
s2c: BEGIN

*on turn start*
s2c: TURN_START <player name>

*on client play card*
c2s: PLAY <card1>, <card2>, ...
  (private)
s2c: NEW_CARDS <new card1>, <new card2>, ...
  (global)
s2c: PLAY <player name> <card1>, <card2>, ...
s2c: PILE_UPDATE <pile top card> <pile size>
s2c: DRAW_PILE_SIZE <size>

*on invalid card play*
s2c: INVALID <err message / reason>
  (client gets to try again, except failed hidden plays)

*on other client card play*
s2c: PLAY <player name> <card1>, <card2>, ...
s2c: PILE_UPDATE <pile top card> <pile size>
s2c: DRAW_PILE_SIZE <size>

*on draw play pile (server enforced)*
s2c: DRAW_ALL <player name>

*on pile clear*
s2c: PILE_CLEAR <player name>

*on win (server enforced)*
s2c: PLAYER_WIN <player name> <placement idx>

*on player quit*
c2s: QUIT
s2c: PLAYER_QUIT <player name>

*on hidden card play*
c2s: PLAY_HIDDEN <index>
s2c: PLAY_HIDDEN <player name> <card1>, <card2>, ...

*on hidden card misplay (server enforced)*
  (server reveals the hidden play, waits ~300ms, then forces draw)
s2c: DRAW_ALL <player name>
s2c: PILE_CLEAR <player name>

DATA_TYPES:
<player name>: String [A-Za-z0-9_ ]{4-20}
<card>:        String <suit><rank>
